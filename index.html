about poga farm

100%
Pasture-Raised Organic

Purity from pasture to your family table

Founded in 2018, Poga Farm was created with a single mission: to revolutionize fresh agricultural produce by combining traditional pasture farming with modern, hygienic, eco-certified liquid and dairy processing.

Our pastured hens and Jersey cows roam freely over 100+ acres of organic clover fields. We never use routine antibiotics, synthetic growth hormones, or chemical additives.
